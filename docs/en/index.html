CantoSpeech — Bilingual Speech-Language Therapy in North York & Toronto

Speech-language therapy for children, teens, and adults in North York and across Toronto. Services available in English, Cantonese, and Mandarin.

At CantoSpeech, we believe every voice has the power to grow. Whether you’re a child building strong communication skills, or a learner discovering the beauty of Cantonese, we’re here to support your journey. Together, let’s play, talk, and grow.
